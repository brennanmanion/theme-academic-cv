The workbook contains large “near me” volumes—such as `sat tutoring near me` (5,400) and `act tutoring near me` (2,400)—but Cassidy's offer is online. Do not build city or “near me” doorway pages without a genuine local presence and location-specific value. A helpful section explaining online tutoring as an alternative to a local search is appropriate; fabricated local pages are not.

- Add two to four contextual internal links per article: up to its service page, across to one closely related guide, and to testimonials where proof is relevant.

## Content brief required for every new page

1. **Search intent:** What decision or problem brings the reader here?
2. **Primary keyword:** One phrase; use it naturally in the title, introduction, and one heading where it reads well.
3. **Supporting terms:** Three to six close variants answered within the same page, not spun into duplicate pages.
4. **Original expertise:** Cassidy's process, examples, advice, or observations that a generic writer cannot supply.
5. **Proof:** Approved testimonials, anonymized outcomes, credentials, or screenshots. Do not invent examples or claims.
6. **Answer-first outline:** Give the direct answer early, then explain options, tradeoffs, process, and FAQs.
7. **Conversion path:** One primary CTA to `/schedule/`; include a secondary link to testimonials or the parent service page.
8. **Metadata:** Unique title and description that describe the page honestly; no comma-separated keyword lists.
9. **Quality check:** Remove repetition, unsupported superlatives, stale year modifiers, and awkward exact-match phrasing.

## Measurement and refresh cadence

- Check indexing and metadata after every deployment.
- Review Search Console query/page performance at 28, 56, and 90 days.
- Track both rankings and business outcomes: clicks to `/schedule/`, completed scheduling actions, and qualified inquiries.
- Refresh pages when query data reveals a missing question or when Cassidy can add stronger first-hand proof.
- Consolidate pages that begin ranking for the same intent instead of publishing another near-duplicate.
