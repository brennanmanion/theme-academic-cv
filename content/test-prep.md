Cassidy provides private SAT and ACT tutoring online for students who want a personalized alternative to a large prep class or self-guided course. Each plan is built around the student’s starting score, target score, test date, schedule, and learning style.

## One-on-one SAT and ACT tutoring online

### Online SAT tutoring

- **SAT Math:** Algebra, advanced math, problem solving, data analysis, and efficient calculator use
- **SAT Reading and Writing:** Comprehension, rhetoric, grammar, vocabulary in context, and evidence-based answers
- **Digital SAT strategy:** Pacing, question triage, educated guessing, and practice with the adaptive test format

Students looking for an SAT tutor online receive focused instruction rather than a generic curriculum. Cassidy can devote a session to a specific need—such as SAT math tutoring online—or combine content review with a complete SAT training plan.

### Online ACT tutoring

- **ACT English and Reading:** Grammar, rhetoric, passage strategy, and time management
- **ACT Math and optional Science:** Core concepts, data interpretation, modeling, and efficient problem-solving
- **ACT test strategy:** Pacing, guessing tactics, endurance, and anxiety reduction

Working with an ACT tutor online gives students the same individualized feedback and accountability without limiting them to tutors in their immediate area.

## How online test prep works

1. **Set the goal.** Cassidy reviews the student’s baseline, score target, test date, and college plans.
2. **Build a personal study plan.** Sessions prioritize the skills and question types most likely to improve the student’s score.
3. **Practice with feedback.** Students learn why an answer works, how to recognize the pattern again, and when to change strategies.
4. **Adjust as scores improve.** Practice-test results guide the next round of lessons and independent work.

Her online SAT coaching and ACT tutoring are customized to each student’s skill gaps, confidence level, and learning preferences. On average, clients see score increases of 200–300+ points on the SAT and 4–6+ points on the ACT.

## Who benefits from private online tutoring?

One-on-one tutoring can be a strong fit for students who:

- Need a structured SAT or ACT study schedule
- Want targeted help in math, English, reading, or science
- Have ADHD, test anxiety, or learning differences
- Have plateaued with self-study or a group program
- Need test prep that works around school, activities, and family schedules

## Frequently asked questions

### Is online SAT tutoring as effective as in-person tutoring?

Online sessions still include live instruction, guided problem-solving, practice review, and an individualized study plan. They also make it easier to work consistently with the same tutor from anywhere.

### Is this a class or private tutoring?

Cassidy’s test prep is one-on-one. The pace, assignments, and lesson topics are chosen for the individual student rather than a group curriculum.

### Can a student prepare for both the SAT and ACT?

Yes. Cassidy can help a student compare diagnostic results, decide which exam better fits their strengths, and build a focused plan for that test.
